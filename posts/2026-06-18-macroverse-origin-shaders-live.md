Promoted to **live** (`main` → `:live`): the **MacroVerse Origin** shader set — six compiling ISF chapters from the Fringe cosmology arc, now in the public starter-pack.

## Live library (`macroverse-origin`)

Filter set **`macroverse-origin`** on [macroverse.aday.net.au](https://macroverse.aday.net.au) for exactly six shaders:

1. Energy Field
2. Particles
3. Blue Giants
4. Orbits
5. Life As We Know It
6. Living Our Best Life

Each has VJ sliders, chapter tags, and Wire-ready ISF headers. Gallery **Seed VJ Sets** (or factory index on fresh containers) assigns `macroverse-origin`, `vj-cosmic`, `vj-ambient`, and `vj-wire-ready`.

## Showcase hero

The [GitHub showcase](https://showcase.macroverse.aday.net.au/) left column now stacks three demo loops under the live shader sliders — no dead space:

- **Pipeline** — GLSL → ISF JSON → `.wire` → Resolume. One binary, no installer.
- **Expose** — magic-number literals → ISF sliders for Wire, one click.
- **Fix chain** — regex → Ollama → AI; stop at first success.

Sliders got larger glowing thumbs with a sparkle pulse on drag.
