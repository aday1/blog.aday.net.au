# MacroVerse at Melbourne Fringe

MacroVerse was a live sonic and visual work at [Melbourne Fringe](https://www.melbournefringe.com.au/whats-on/events/macroverse), part of Abbotsford Convent's experimental art program.

Reductionist (Nick Wilson) performed with battery-powered micro-instruments and micro-virtuosic technique, carrying a macro narrative of the universe from energy stasis through heat-death dissipation. Aday (Adrian Richardson) provided live video projection improv that distorted time and manipulated perceived energy flow.

Both artists are longtime members of Australia's Clan Analogue collective. The exhibition ran free daily from 12pm to 4pm; ticketed performance sessions ran from 7pm each night for about one hour, alongside related contemporary works on the same program.

The browser stack at macroverse.aday.net.au extends that live-visual practice into a GLSL/ISF shader engine for VJ and projection work.
