ArtBastard **5.2.15.0** adds an operator-focused seed pack for APC rows 3-5 so you can layer aim and color over Essential 14+14 while keeping dimmers and strobe under manual control.

---

## What changed

- **Operator rows 3-5 (A and B)** fills APC slots 17-40 on both decks (48 scenes total).
- **Row 3 (17-24):** pan/tilt only on movers — static positions plus optional sweep/90 animations.
- **Row 4 (25-32):** color only — mix with row 1-2 looks on Deck A and B.
- **Row 5 (33-40):** color + pan/tilt — never writes gobo, strobe, dimmer, or shutter.
- **Include automation** checkbox now appears in pack mode for sweeps and color cycles.
- **SuperControl** saved scenes list scrolls in a compact column instead of stretching the page.

Suggested workflow: seed Essential 14+14 first (slots 01-14), then Operator rows 3-5, then dial dimmers and strobe yourself on the desk.
